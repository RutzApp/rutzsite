FAQ

What is Rutz?

Rutz is an AI adaptive running coach. It builds flexible weekly running plans that level with you and adapt to place and time — instead of locking you into a rigid pre-built schedule.

How is this different from a static training plan?

Static plans assume perfect attendance. Rutz is built for real weeks: when you miss a session or travel, the plan is meant to flex rather than leave you “behind.”

Do I need Garmin or Apple Watch to join?

The waitlist landing’s connection story is Intervals.icu. Watches you already sync into Intervals.icu are a data path through that bridge — via Intervals.icu, not a direct Garmin or Apple Watch login on Rutz.

Is there an App Store or TestFlight link?

Not yet. Join the waitlist and we’ll notify you when access is available.

What is Intervals.icu’s role?

Intervals.icu is the account bridge for connecting training data. Rutz is not claiming to be the only AI tool in the Intervals.icu ecosystem.

Is Rutz a Runna / TrainAsONE alternative?

Rutz targets runners who want an adaptive, flexible weekly plan and are fine starting on a waitlist. We don’t claim feature parity or “#1” rankings.
