What is a marathon training app?

A marathon training app is a mobile app that guides you through the 12 to 20 weeks of preparation for a 42.195 km race. It schedules your long runs, easy runs and quality sessions, tracks what you complete, and usually syncs with a GPS watch.

Marathon blocks are long, and almost nobody gets through one without illness, travel or a bad week. That is where a marathon training app earns its keep: a static plan leaves you behind schedule, while an adaptive app rebuilds the road to race day from where you actually are.

Rutz keeps your race date and phases fixed and rewrites the individual weeks. A load guard caps how quickly your volume can grow, so a missed week is absorbed — never crammed back in.

FAQ: Marathon training app

What is the best marathon training app?

The best marathon training app is the one whose plan you will actually follow for 16 weeks. Look for personalization from your own data, adaptation when you miss training, and a sensible cap on how fast your mileage builds. Rutz is designed around exactly those three; it is currently iPhone-only and in private beta.

How many weeks does a marathon training app plan for?

Typically 12 to 20 weeks. Rutz works backwards from your race date and sizes the base, build, peak and taper phases to the time you have.

Can I use a marathon training app for my first marathon?

Yes. First-timers benefit most from a longer, 20-week build and from having every run explained. Make sure you can comfortably run 10K before you start.

What happens to my marathon plan if I get injured or ill?

See a medical professional first. When you return, Rutz rebuilds from your current fitness rather than resuming where the calendar says you should be, and limits how quickly your load ramps back up.

Does it work with my Garmin or COROS watch?

Yes. Any watch that syncs to Intervals.icu works with Rutz, including Garmin, COROS, Suunto and Polar.
