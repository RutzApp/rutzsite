What is a running training plan app?

A running training plan app is a mobile app that tells you what to run and when, in order to reach a goal — usually a race distance or a finish time. Instead of following a static PDF, you get a schedule on your phone that syncs with your watch and tracks what you complete.

The best running training plan apps do three things: they personalize the plan to your fitness and calendar, they adapt when your training doesn't go to plan, and they explain what your runs mean. Many apps do the first. Few do all three.

Rutz is built around the second and third. Your race date and training phases stay fixed, but each week is written fresh from the runs you actually did. After every run you get a coach's read on how it went and what it changes.

What to look for in a running training plan app

Real personalization

Does it use your own run history and heart-rate data, or just ask your age and a recent race time?

Adaptation

When you miss a week, does the plan change — or does it just mark you as behind?

Explanation

Does it tell you why a run matters and how the last one went, or only hand you numbers?

Safe progression

Is there a limit on how fast your training load can climb? Most running injuries are load errors.

Device support

Does it work with the watch you already own? Rutz reads Garmin, COROS, Suunto, Polar and more through Intervals.icu.

Honest pricing

Can you try it before you commit? The Rutz waitlist is free, and inviting friends earns free premium time.

FAQ: Running training plan app

What is the best app for running training plans?

It depends on what you need. If you want a large library of fixed plans and guided audio workouts on iOS and Android, established apps like Runna or Nike Run Club are strong choices. If you want a plan that is rewritten each week from your actual runs, with AI analysis of every run and a coach you can chat to, Rutz is built for that. Rutz is currently iPhone-only and in private beta.

Is there an AI running coach app?

Yes. Rutz is an AI running coach app. It reads your watch data through Intervals.icu, computes your training load, pacing and efficiency in code, and uses AI to interpret those results into a weekly plan and a plain-language review of each run.

Does Rutz work with Garmin?

Yes, through Intervals.icu. Connect your Garmin account to Intervals.icu (it's free), then sign in to Rutz with Intervals.icu. COROS, Suunto, Polar, Wahoo and other devices that sync to Intervals.icu work the same way.

Is Rutz free?

Joining the waitlist is free. Final pricing hasn't been announced. Waitlist members who invite friends earn free premium time.

Is Rutz on Android?

Not yet. Rutz is an iPhone app. Join the waitlist and tell us your device in the survey — it directly shapes what we build next.

Can a running app replace a human coach?

For most recreational runners, a good adaptive app covers what matters: structure, progression, feedback and accountability. A human coach is still the better choice for elite goals, complex injury history, or if you simply want a person in your corner.
