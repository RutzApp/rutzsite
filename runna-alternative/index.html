Rutz vs Runna

Rutz and Runna are both running training plan apps. Runna gives you a coach-designed plan from a large library; Rutz writes your plan one week at a time from the runs you actually did. Here is an honest, feature-by-feature comparison.

Which one?

Pick the app that fits how you train

Choose Runna if…

You want an app you can download today, on iOS or Android, with a deep plan library, strength and mobility sessions, and plans designed by human coaches.

Choose Rutz if…

You want a plan that is rebuilt every week from what you really ran, a written analysis of every run, and an AI coach you can question — and you're happy to join a waitlist on iPhone.

Use both?

Nothing stops you. Follow a structured plan elsewhere and use Rutz for the per-run analysis and load tracking, as long as your runs reach Intervals.icu.

FAQ: Rutz vs Runna

Is Rutz a Runna alternative?

Yes, for runners who want an adaptive plan and AI run analysis. Both apps build personalized running training plans. Runna offers a large library of coach-designed plans on iOS and Android. Rutz writes your plan one week at a time from your actual training and reviews every run; it is iPhone-only and currently in private beta.

What are the best alternatives to Runna?

Popular running training plan apps include Runna, Nike Run Club, TrainAsONE, Garmin Coach, TrainingPeaks and Rutz. They differ mainly in how plans adapt, which devices they support, whether human coaches are involved, and price.

Is Rutz cheaper than Runna?

Rutz pricing hasn't been announced. Joining the waitlist is free and referrals earn free premium time. Runna listed $19.99 per month or $119.99 per year when we checked in September 2026.

Does Rutz have strength training like Runna?

Not yet. Rutz is focused on running: the plan, the per-run analysis and training load. If guided strength and Pilates sessions are important to you, Runna is the better fit today.

Can I move from Runna to Rutz?

Yes. Rutz reads your run history from Intervals.icu, so as long as your watch syncs there, your past training comes with you and the first week is written from it.
