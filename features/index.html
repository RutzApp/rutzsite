Explore what Rutz can do

Rutz is an AI running coach for iPhone. It writes your training plan one week at a time, reviews every run, answers your questions and keeps your training load in check.

Join the waitlist

Adaptive plan

Your plan. Your week.

Give Rutz your race and your date. It lays out the phases — base, build, peak, taper — then writes one week at a time from the runs you actually did.

  • Built backwards from race day
  • Rewritten every week from real training
  • Missed runs absorbed, never crammed back in

Run analysis

Every run, explained.

Code does the math; your coach does the interpreting. Pacing, heart rate, efficiency and fatigue are computed from your data, then turned into a review you can read in thirty seconds.

  • A written review after every run
  • Segments, reps and grade-adjusted pace
  • What it means for tomorrow

AI coach

Ask anything. Get a straight answer.

Should I race this weekend? Why was today so hard? Your coach has read your whole history, so the answer is about you — not runners in general.

  • Chat that knows your training
  • Plain language, no jargon
  • Answers in your language

Load guard

Build without boiling over.

Most running injuries are training-load errors. Rutz tracks how much you're carrying and caps how fast it can climb, so ambition never outruns your legs.

  • Week-to-week ramp ceilings
  • Fitness, fatigue and form trends
  • Easier weeks when you need them

Four steps

How it works

  1. 01

    Join the waitlist

    Rutz is in private beta on iPhone. Save your spot and we'll email you when it's your turn.

  2. 02

    Set your goal

    Tell us the race, the date and how many days you can run. First 5K or a marathon PB — both count.

  3. 03

    Start training

    Sign in with Intervals.icu. Your history loads, your first week lands, and every run after it gets a coach's read.

  4. 04

    Show up ready

    Each week is written from the last one. You get fitter, the plan gets sharper, race day gets closer.
